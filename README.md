# E-commerce Sales Performance Analysis

## Overview
An exploratory analysis of e-commerce sales data using Microsoft Excel, 
identifying top performing categories, regions, and customer segments.

## Tool
Microsoft Excel

## Dataset
Superstore Sales Dataset — 9,800 rows of retail transaction data
covering orders from 2015 to 2018.

## Business Questions Answered
- Which product category generates the most revenue?
- Which region performs best?
- What does the monthly sales trend look like?
- Which customer segment places the most orders?
- Which sub-category is the top performer?

## Key Findings
1. Technology is the highest revenue category, generating $376,604.82, 
   accounting for approximately 36% of overall revenue.
2. The West region is the best performing region with total sales of 
   $325,811.33, while the South recorded the lowest sales.
3. Sales show a clear seasonal pattern between 2015 and 2018, with 
   consistent spikes towards the end of each year, indicating strong 
   holiday season demand.
4. The Consumer segment is the most active customer group, accounting 
   for 52% of total orders (2,535 orders).
5. Chairs is the top performing sub-category with total sales of 
   $162,161.63, followed by Phones and Machines.
